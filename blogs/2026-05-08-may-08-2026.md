Feature Storage Transfer Service now supports AWS GovCloud (US) regions, including us-gov-east-1 and us-gov-west-1 . You can now transfer data from Amazon S3 buckets located in GovCloud regions using both batch and event-driven transfers. For more information, see Configure access to a source: Amazon S3 and Event-driven transfers from AWS S3 .
